About Kaiteki

Accessibility should feel human.

Founded in 2022, Kaiteki Innovations was built on a simple belief: accessibility should be part of everyday infrastructure, not an expensive retrofit only a few can afford.

Across homes, hospitals, institutions, and public spaces, millions of people continue to navigate environments that were not designed for independent use. Kaiteki combines lived experience, assistive technology, sanitation design, and practical deployment to close that gap.

Kaiteki Innovations Private Limited is a DPIIT-recognized startup registered under RoC Hyderabad, working across healthcare accessibility, inclusive infrastructure, and sanitation innovation.

The access gap

Basic sanitation is still inaccessible for too many people.

For persons with disabilities, elderly individuals, rehabilitation patients, and caregivers, using a restroom can become physically exhausting and emotionally distressing.

Existing solutions are often expensive, difficult to install, or dependent on major structural modifications. Kaiteki bridges this gap with retrofit-friendly systems designed for real-world environments.

Meet GUKO

A patented non-electric bidet system for independent hygiene accessibility.

GUKO retrofits directly onto existing western-style toilets without additional space or structural modifications.

  • Dual accessibility: hand actuator and foot pedal options for different mobility needs.
  • Non-electric system: mechanical water-pressure design requiring zero electricity.
  • Retrofit friendly: made for homes, hospitals, institutions, public infrastructure, and event deployments.
